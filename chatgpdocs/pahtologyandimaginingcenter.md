## FINAL DESIGN RULE

Pathology Management System ka central workflow hoga:

**Patient → Test Order → Sample Collection → Sample Tracking → Processing → Result Entry → Professional Verification → Final Report → Delivery → Billing / Accounting Records**

Patient, test order, specimen, result, report, invoice aur payment ko properly linked entities rakho. Har sample aur har test ki traceability honi chahiye.

Is tarah aapka Pathology / Laboratory Management System ek standalone diagnostic laboratory se lekar hospital ke large central laboratory tak scale kar sakta hai.

Bhai, ab doosra **Complete Radiology / Imaging Management System** hai. Ismein sirf MRI, X-Ray, CT Scan, Ultrasound, Doppler, Mammography aur doosri imaging services hongi. Pathology / Laboratory is module se completely separate rahega.

# COMPLETE RADIOLOGY / IMAGING MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

**Coverage:** MRI, X-Ray, CT Scan, Ultrasound, Doppler, Mammography, OPG / Dental Imaging, Fluoroscopy aur doosri diagnostic imaging services.

Is system mein patient registration, imaging orders, appointments, patient preparation, imaging procedures, DICOM/PACS, radiology reporting, billing, payments, equipment management, staff, quality, notifications aur reports sab cover honge.

# FINAL DESIGN RULE

Radiology / Imaging Management System ka central workflow hoga:

**Patient → Imaging Order → Appointment → Preparation → Imaging Study → DICOM / PACS → Radiologist Review → Final Report → Report Delivery → Billing / Accounting Records**

Patient, imaging order, appointment, imaging study, DICOM reference, radiology report, invoice aur payment ko properly linked entities rakho.

MRI, X-Ray, CT, Ultrasound aur doosri imaging services ek hi Radiology / Imaging module ke andar rahengi, lekin har modality ki specific scheduling, preparation, equipment aur procedural checklists configurable hongi.

**Pathology / Laboratory ka sample collection, CBC, LFT, KFT, laboratory analyzer aur pathology reporting workflow is module ka part nahi hoga.**


Bhai, Radiology / Imaging Management System mein bhi **har role ka alag dashboard, sidebar, permissions aur work queue** honi chahiye. Neeche sirf Radiology / Imaging ke roles diye hain—Pathology / Laboratory ke roles ismein include nahi hain.
