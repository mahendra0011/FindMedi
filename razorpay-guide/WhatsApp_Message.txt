*💳 Razorpay Payment Gateway Setup Guide (Website/App ke liye)*

Namaste! 🙏
Website me online payment accept karne ke liye Razorpay ka account setup karna hota hai. Yeh bahut aasan hai, niche diye steps follow karein:

*1. Account Kahan Banayein?*
👉 Website: https://razorpay.com par jayein aur *Sign Up* karein.
(Koi app download karne ki zaroorat nahi hai, seedha browser se ho jayega.)

*2. Bank & KYC Documents kya lagte hain?*
• Bank Account Number & IFSC (Jisme customer ke paise aayenge)
• PAN Card
• Aadhaar Card
• Cancelled Cheque ya Bank Statement

*3. Website par zaroori Pages (Approval ke liye):*
Razorpay approve karne se pehle website dekhta hai, isliye website par yeh pages hone zaroori hain:
• About Us
• Contact Us (Email, Phone, Address)
• Terms & Conditions
• Privacy Policy
• Refund/Cancellation Policy

*4. Developer ko kya share karna hai?*
Login karne ke baad:
👉 *Dashboard -> Settings -> API Keys -> Generate Key*
Wahan se 2 keys milti hain:
1. *Key ID*
2. *Key Secret*
Yeh dono keys developer ko share karni hain taaki wo website me link kar sakein.

*5. Paise Bank me kaise aate hain?*
• Customer website par UPI/Card/NetBanking se pay karega.
• Paise turant aapke Razorpay dashboard me dikhenge.
• 1-2 din ke andar (T+2 days) automatically aapke bank account me credit ho jate hain (aapko manually kuch nahi karna padta).

Agar setup ke dauran koi bhi doubt ho toh call/message kar sakte hain! 👍
