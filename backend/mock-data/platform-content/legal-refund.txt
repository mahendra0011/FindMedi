Cancellation & Refund Policy Fair, transparent cancellation guidelines across appointments, emergency runs & pharmacy Back to Home 1. In-Clinic & Telemedicine Doctor Appointments Cancellation &gt; 2 Hours Prior to Slot: 100% full refund is automatically credited back to your source payment method or instant MediCoins wallet without any cancellation penalty. Cancellation &lt; 2 Hours Prior to Slot: 50% refund is issued, with the remaining 50% retained as clinic slot blocking compensation, unless rescheduled to another available slot within 7 days. Doctor Cancellation or No-Show: If the attending physician cancels the appointment or is unavailable, you receive a 100% automatic refund plus a priority rebooking credit. 2. Emergency Flying Doctor & Ambulance SOS Dispatches Because emergency dispatches trigger immediate vehicle mobilization, specialized cancellation rules apply to protect on-duty responders: Cancellation While Searching (0 – 120s): If cancelled while the platform is still searching for candidate doctors/ambulances, 100% of pre-authorized funds are immediately released with zero fee. Emergency Doctor Standby Fee (Post-Claim Transit): If a patient cancels after an emergency doctor has accepted the dispatch and commenced physical road transit, a standardized ₹250 standby compensation fee is charged to cover transit fuel and doctor readiness. The remaining balance is refunded. Ambulance En-Route Cancellation: Once an ICU/BLS ambulance has rolled out of the hospital bay, a base mobilization charge applies if cancelled by the caller before arrival. 3. Diagnostic Tests & Health Checkup Packages Prior to Sample Collection: 100% refund if cancelled at least 1 hour before scheduled home sample collection or diagnostic center visit. Post Sample Collection: Once the phlebotomist has collected biological specimens or imaging scans have commenced, no refund can be processed as laboratory consumables and reagent testing are committed. 4. Pharmacy Orders & Prescription Medicine Returns Under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 , strict quality regulations govern medicine returns: Eligible for 48h Return Unopened, undamaged medicine strips, sealed bottles, and diagnostic equipment in original manufacturer packaging with intact batch numbers. Strictly Non-Returnable Cold-chain biologics (Insulin, Vaccines), opened syrups, cut blister strips, Schedule X controlled substances, and hygiene goods. 5. Refund Processing Timelines & Modes All approved refunds are initiated instantly by our automated billing engine.
