National Medical Commission (NMC) Telemedicine Framework Every digital consultation (via video stream, audio call, or encrypted chat) conducted over FindMedi is governed by the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines (March 25, 2020) issued by the Board of Governors in supersession of the Medical Council of India (MCI) and notified by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW), Government of India. 2. Patient Informed Consent Acknowledgments By initiating or booking an online teleconsultation, the patient or legal guardian expressly acknowledges and consents to the following clinical principles: Clinical Limitations: A virtual teleconsultation relies on audio-visual feeds and patient-reported symptoms. It does not allow for physical palpation, chest auscultation with an acoustic stethoscope, or invasive diagnostic testing. Practitioner Clinical Discretion: If the attending medical practitioner determines that teleconsultation is inadequate to reach a safe diagnosis, they have the statutory obligation to recommend an in-person clinic visit, emergency flying doctor dispatch, or hospital OPD evaluation. Explicit Consent: Explicit patient consent is recorded when the patient clicks "Join Call" or initiates the consultation session. 3. Digital Prescription Rules & Prohibited Drugs (List O, A, B, and Prohibited List) Medical practitioners on FindMedi prescribe medications strictly in accordance with the drug categories sanctioned under the NMC Guidelines: Permissible Medications (List O, A, and B): Over-the-counter (OTC) formulations, first-aid remedies, re-fill maintenance medications for previously diagnosed chronic illnesses (hypertension, diabetes, asthma), and add-on therapies. Strictly Prohibited Online (Schedule X & Narcotics): Medicines listed under Schedule X of the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945 , and all substances governed by the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, 1985 (including potent hypnotics, sedatives, morphine derivatives, and habit-forming psychotropics) are STRICTLY BANNED from being prescribed via teleconsultation. Any attempt to solicit such prescriptions will result in immediate session termination. 4. Legal Validity of FindMedi Digital Prescriptions Prescriptions generated through the FindMedi clinical engine comply with the electronic record standards of Section 4 and Section 5 of the Information Technology Act, 2000 . Each digital prescription contains: Doctor's full name, medical qualification, and State Medical Council / NMC Registration Number. Patient name, age, biological gender, and date of consultation. Generic drug names with explicit dosage, route, frequency, and duration of therapy.
