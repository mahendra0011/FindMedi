Acute Life-Threatening Emergencies (Dial 112 / 108) FindMedi is NOT an alternative to emergency hospital trauma centers or intensive care resuscitation . If you or any individual around you is experiencing life-threatening symptoms—such as acute myocardial infarction (heart attack), stroke, severe hemorrhage, respiratory arrest, anaphylactic shock, or traumatic vehicular injury—you must immediately contact the national emergency hotlines: Dial 108 (National Ambulance) Dial 112 (All-India Emergency) 2. Technology Intermediary Status (IT Act Sec 79) FindMedi operates solely as a digital healthcare coordination platform and an Intermediary under Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000 . FindMedi does not practice medicine, operate hospitals, or directly provide medical diagnoses. All medical treatments, flying squad bedside interventions, telemedicine consultations, laboratory pathology analyses, and prescription drugs are delivered by independent, verified third-party registered medical practitioners (RMPs), licensed hospitals, accredited diagnostic labs, and retail pharmacies. 3. Good Samaritan Legal Shield (Motor Vehicles Act Sec 134A) In strict accordance with Section 134A of the Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act 2019 and the guidelines formulated by the Hon’ble Supreme Court of India in SaveLIFE Foundation v. Union of India : Any citizen or bystander who uses FindMedi to report an emergency or request an ambulance/flying doctor for a road accident victim or stranger in distress is fully protected from civil and criminal liability. Good Samaritans shall not be detained at hospitals, compelled to disclose personal identity, or forced to bear treatment/transport costs for the victim. Responding flying squad physicians providing emergency bedside stabilization in good faith are granted full statutory protection from unwarranted harassment. 4. Penalties for False Alarms & Prank Emergency Dispatches Triggering a frivolous, hoax, or prank Emergency Doctor or Ambulance SOS dispatch wastes critical life-safety resources and endangers patients genuinely fighting for survival. ⚖️ Statutory Criminal Notice: Transmitting false emergency alarms or intentionally misleading first responders constitutes an offense under Section 182 and Section 268 of the Indian Penal Code (IPC) / Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) , punishable by imprisonment and monetary fines. FindMedi logs IP telemetry, verified phone numbers, and GPS coordinates and cooperates fully with law enforcement agencies in prosecuting false distress reports. 5. Mental Health Crisis & Suicide Intervention (Mental Healthcare Act 2017) FindMedi chat consultations and wellness tools are not designed for acute psychiatric emergencies or active suicide intervention.
