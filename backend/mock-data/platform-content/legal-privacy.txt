Commitment to Health Data Security FindMedi is deeply committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of your personal information and sensitive personal health data (SPHD). In full compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and the Information Technology Act 2000, all medical records, diagnostics, doctor notes, and teleconsultation audio/video streams are protected by state-of-the-art encryption protocols. 2. Information We Collect We collect information that you provide directly to us when registering, booking medical consultations, or purchasing medications: Demographic Information: Full name, date of birth, biological gender, mobile number, and residential address. Clinical Information: Medical history, allergy profiles, blood group, vital signs, prescriptions, diagnostic reports, and ABHA ID. Telemetry & Geolocation: Real-time GPS coordinates collected during active emergency ambulance dispatches, ride requests, and medicine deliveries. Financial Information: Payment tokens processed via RBI-authorized payment aggregators (Razorpay, Stripe); we never store raw credit card numbers or CVV. 3. Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) & ABHA Consent FindMedi functions as an approved Health Information User (HIU) and Health Information Provider (HIP) under the ABDM ecosystem. Your longitudinal health records are shared with authorized healthcare facilities strictly on a consent-based architecture. You retain the absolute right to revoke data consent, request audit trails of health record access, or unlink your ABHA number at any time via your Patient Settings. 4. Emergency Flying Doctor & Real-Time Telemetry Privacy During critical acute medical emergencies triggered via the Emergency Doctor SOS or Ambulance SOS : Patient GPS & Triage Telemetry: Precise GPS coordinates, emergency crisis category, and reported vital symptoms are broadcast exclusively to verified on-duty physicians within a 5–15 km operational perimeter to facilitate immediate physical arrival. Doctor On-Duty GPS Beacons: When a clinic doctor activates Emergency Flying Squad Duty , background GPS beacons are streamed at 5-second intervals to calculate accurate ETAs and route dispatch telemetry. Doctor location streaming terminates automatically upon standing down from duty. Emergency Medical Summary Sharing: Known allergies, chronic conditions, and current medications are disclosed to the dispatched physician under the emergency life-safety exemption of Section 7 of the DPDP Act 2023. Telemetry Auto-Purge: High-frequency transient GPS telemetry pings are scrubbed within 30 days of case completion; only the final verified clinical summary and invoice are retained in the patient's encrypted health vault. 5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Protections We do not sell, rent, or monetize your health data under any circumstances. Information is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis: With Assigned Practitioners: To facilitate clinical diagnosis, prescription writing, and treatment planning. With Logistics Partners: Delivery couriers receive only delivery address, recipient name, and masked phone numbers. Statutory Authorities: Disclosed only when strictly required by a court order, police FIR, or public health epidemic notification mandate. 5.1 Provider Tiering & Hospital Fine-Grained Access Control FindMedi enforces zero-trust, fine-grained access control within all healthcare tenant organizations: Provider Regulatory Tiers: Organizations are strictly partitioned. Tier 1 (Hospitals, Clinics, Doctors) process primary health records; Tier 2 (Diagnostic Labs, Pharmacies) process lab orders and medicine dispensing; Tier 3 (Gyms, Fitness, Wellness, Stores) are technically blocked from accessing any clinical diagnoses, prescriptions, or patient medical histories. Ward & Department Boundary Isolation: In hospital settings, access is restricted to operational scopes (e.g. Ward-3 nurses can only record vitals for beds in Ward-3; treating doctors only access charts of patients under their active care). Restricted Category Safeguards: Highly sensitive medical categories (mental health therapy, HIV/STD, substance use, sexual health) require explicit justification reason codes and step-up verification. Patient Access Logs: Any emergency or break-glass access outside the treating care-team generates a transparent log entry visible in the patient's Privacy Centre. 6. Your Rights as a Data Principal Under Indian law, you have the right to access your health data in a portable format (JSON/CSV), correct inaccurate health history, nominate emergency representatives to manage records, or request complete account erasure.
