Redressal Service Level Agreement (SLA) Timelines Acknowledgment within 24 Hours Every formal complaint submitted via email or portal generates a unique Ticket ID and is acknowledged within twenty-four (24) hours. Resolution within 15 Days Comprehensive investigation, consultation with attending medical facilities, and final written resolution are delivered within fifteen (15) days. 4. Dispute Escalation Hierarchy If your grievance is not resolved satisfactorily within the statutory fifteen (15) day period: Data Protection Inquiries: You retain the statutory right to file a complaint before the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) under Section 27 of the DPDP Act 2023. Consumer Service Disputes: You may register an online complaint with the National Consumer Helpline (NCH) at consumerhelpline.gov.in (Toll-Free 1915). Medical Clinical Malpractice: Clinical negligence allegations are cognizable by the Ethics and Medical Registration Board of the National Medical Commission (NMC) or the relevant State Medical Council. © FindMedi Technologies Pvt.
