## 1. Terms of Service (Users) — Draft

A "Verified" badge means only the documents/scope stated in its description were checked on the stated date.

**1.6 Bookings & payments.** Prices are shown before payment, inclusive/exclusive of taxes as indicated. Payments are processed by third-party gateways. **1.10 Acceptable use.** No fake bookings, impersonation, harassment, scraping, reverse-engineering, circumventing security, uploading malware, misuse of coupons/referrals, or booking on behalf of others without authority.

**Enforcement:** warning → content removal → suspension → ban; appeal available; severe cases (child safety, threats) reported to authorities.
**Reviews:** only after completed bookings; one per booking; edit window `[ ]` days; provider may reply; reviews not removed for being negative.

## 7. Advertising & Claims Policy — Draft
**Prohibited:** guaranteed cure/results; miracle claims; cure claims for diseases covered by the *Drugs & Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act*; misleading before/after; fake testimonials; unverified "best/No.1/100%"; comparison disparaging others; sex-determination (PCPNDT); unapproved drug/device claims; targeting children with health claims.
**Required:** accurate qualifications/registration; price transparency; "Sponsored" labelling; consent for patient images; disclaimers for cosmetic/wellness; professional advertising rules (NMC/state councils/Bar Council where applicable).
**Review & takedown:** automated + manual review; sensitive categories flagged `adClaimsRestricted`; repeat violations → suspension.
