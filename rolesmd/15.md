# 14 — Implementation Roadmap (Sprint-wise Plan)

> **Important:** neeche ke time estimates **rough assumptions** hain, commitments nahi. Aapki asli team size, velocity aur existing code quality se badlenge. Pehle 1–2 sprint ke baad **velocity measure karke** poora plan re-estimate karo.
>
> **Assumed team (adjust karo):** 2 backend · 2 frontend · 1 product designer · 1 QA/SDET · 1 PM/founder · 0.5 DevOps · part-time lawyer/CA/clinical advisor.
> **Sprint = 2 weeks.** "S" = Small (≤ 3 days), "M" = Medium (≤ 1 sprint), "L" = Large (2–3 sprints), "XL" = needs breakdown.

---

## 0. Scope reality check (padhna zaruri hai)

Docs me 25+ provider types, 24 naye roles, 7 flow types hain. Ye **saari cheezein ek saath nahi** banengi. Rules:
1. **Ek city + 2–3 categories** se launch (suggestion: Dental, Dietitian/AYUSH, Home sample collection ya Equipment rental — jahan demand ho aur regulatory friction kam ho; local validation se confirm karo).
2. **Pehle foundation, phir features.** Foundation ke bina har naya type duplicate kaam hoga.
3. **Har phase ke end par decision gate** (data dekho: kya providers aaye? bookings hui? quality?).
4. **"Kya NAHI banana" list** rakho (§9).
5. Sensitive/regulated categories (IVF, de-addiction, palliative, funeral, organ pledge) **R4** me, pentest + legal review ke baad.

## 1. Phases at a glance

| Phase | Naam | Duration (rough) | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R-1** | Stabilise & Secure | 1–2 sprints | `.env` leak fixed, prod env hardened, CI security, tests green |

Total (agar sab karo): **~5–7 mahine R-1 se R2 tak**, R3–R4 aur baad me. Real timeline velocity par depend.

## 12. Risk register (top)

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Scope creep | Delays | Gates, "not now" list, 1-city focus |
| Regulatory misstep (Rx, claims, telemedicine) | Legal/safety | Lawyer + clinical advisor; addenda; moderation |
| Supply too thin | Demand drop | Field ops seeding, govt listings, camps, partnerships |
| Payment/refund bugs | Trust/revenue | Idempotency, reconciliation, staged rollout, monitoring |
| Data breach | Severe | P0 security, field encryption, audits, incident drills |
| Team burnout / bus factor | Delays | Docs, pair on critical systems, CODEOWNERS |
| Poor provider adoption (complex dashboards) | Churn | Simple v1, training, WhatsApp support, mobile-first |
| Low-quality providers | Brand damage | KYC, probation, reviews integrity, strikes |
| Cost overrun (infra/SMS/WhatsApp) | Margin | Budgets/alerts (file 19), batching, channel mix |
| Over-reliance on vendors | Outage | Fallbacks, SLAs, status monitoring |

## 13. Cadence & governance
Weekly demo · fortnightly planning/retro · monthly metrics review & roadmap re-prioritisation · quarterly security/compliance review (access review, key rotation, restore drill) · release train (staging → canary → prod) · incident postmortems (blameless).
