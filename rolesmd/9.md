# 09 — Jo Cheezein Chhut Sakti Hain (Missing Pieces & Cross-Cutting)

> Aapne jo pucha (join, cards, detail, profile, flows, dashboards) uske alawa ek poore health marketplace ko chalane ke liye ye cheezein bhi chahiye. Har item: **kyun zaruri** + **kya banana hai** + **priority**.
> Priority: **P0** launch se pehle · **P1** pehle 3 mahine · **P2** baad me.

---

## 1. Discovery

### 1.1 Unified search (P0)
- Ek search bar: doctors, hospitals, clinics, labs, tests, medicines, packages, dentists, yoga, gyms, dietitians, equipment, events, symptoms/conditions
- **Typeahead** + categories + recent + trending; **spell-tolerant** (Orthopedics = Orthopaedics = "hadda ka doctor"); **Hinglish/Hindi/regional + voice**
- **Synonyms/aliases** from taxonomy; symptom → specialty mapping ("tooth pain" → dentist, "seene me dard" → cardiology/emergency note)
- Ranking: relevance, distance, availability, rating, verification, **paid placement clearly labelled**, no pay-to-hide-bad-reviews
- Filters/facets (city, area, open now, home visit, online, govt/private, scheme accepted, price, language, gender, accessibility)
- **No-result handling:** nearby alternatives, "request this service", unmet-demand log → ops dashboard
- Tech: OpenSearch ✅ (already in stack) — index public DTOs only; redaction ✅; tenant filters server-side.

### 1.2 Map view & nearby (P1)
Map with pins/cards, "near me" (consent), radius, route/ETA, open-now layer; self-hosted routing ✅ (Valhalla); no exact home addresses for home-based providers.

### 1.3 Home/landing & SEO (P0)
Category tiles, city selector, trending, offers, camps/events, emergency strip. SEO: city + category pages, schema.org markup, sitemap, content hub, no PHI in URLs/titles.

### 1.4 Compare & shortlist (P2)
Compare 2–3 providers (fees, schemes, ratings, distance, facilities); saved lists/collections.

## 6. Privacy, consent & data rights (P0)
- **Consent management:** granular, purpose-wise, withdrawable, versioned; ABDM consent ✅
- **Privacy centre** for users (file 06) + **data-subject requests** (access/correct/erase/export) with SLAs
- **Children's data** (<18): verifiable guardian consent (DPDP), no tracking/ads targeting
- **Retention & deletion** ✅ docs; automate purge + backups expiry
- **Sensitive categories:** discreet display/notifications, separate consent, hidden from suggestions/recents
- **Cross-border/vendor** disclosure: Sentry/PostHog/Unleash/LiveKit/Render in DPIA ✅ to-do
- **Provider-side privacy:** need-to-know access, patient-consent for sharing, access logs visible to patients
- Legal pages: ToS, Privacy Policy, Refund/Cancellation, Provider Agreement, Community Guidelines, Grievance Redressal (officer details), Disclaimer.

## 16. Business & monetisation models (decide early)
| Model | Notes |
|---|---|
| Commission per booking/order ✅ | Config by type/city/plan |
| Subscription for providers ✅ (free/basic/premium) | Featured listings, analytics, staff seats |
| Featured/sponsored ✅ | Labelled; policy limits |
| Lead-gen (request & quote) | Fee per qualified lead; avoid pay-for-medical-referral issues (kickbacks prohibited — fee-splitting rules; legal check) |
| Memberships (patients) | Health-plus plan: discounts, priority, free consults |
| B2B | Corporate wellness, school health, insurer partnerships |
| Marketplace products | Margin on supplements/devices (compliance heavy) |
| Events | Ticketing fee/sponsorships |
| Data | **Never sell PHI**; aggregated insights only with consent/anonymisation |
⚠️ **Fee-splitting/commission with doctors:** Indian medical ethics rules restrict doctor referral commissions; structure platform fees as technology/listing/service fees and get legal review.
