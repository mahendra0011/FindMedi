# 08 — Admin, Superadmin & Moderation (Ops Console)

> **Existing (✅) superadmin pages (examples):** KYC (`KycCommand`), licences (`License`), approvals (`PendingApproval`), categories (`Category` admin), disputes, grievances, KPIs, moderation, announcements, featured listings, platform coupons, commission config, city/service-city, audit log, billing, content (`PlatformContent`).
> **Goal:** naye 25+ provider types, products, events aur UGC ko safe tarah se operate karne ke liye ops console ko extend karna.

---

## 1. Console structure

```
Dashboard · Onboarding & KYC · Providers · Catalog & Taxonomy · Content & Moderation · Orders & Bookings · Payments & Finance
· Disputes & Support · Trust & Safety · Marketing · Analytics · Compliance & Legal · Platform Settings · Audit & Security
```
Role-based: `superadmin`, `kyc_reviewer`, `compliance_officer`, `catalog_manager`, `moderator`, `support_agent`, `finance_admin`, `content_editor/medical_reviewer`, `city_manager`, `analyst`. **No single "god mode"** — sensitive actions need step-up + dual approval.

## 2. Onboarding & KYC queue (extends ✅ KycCommand/PendingApproval)

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Queues by type & priority** | Clinical (T1), Commerce, Wellness, Home services, Transport, Events, Professional; high-risk lane (ambulance, IVF, de-addiction, peer mentors) with **two-person approval** |
| **Review workspace** | Applicant data, docs viewer (watermarked, no download for reviewers unless permitted), type-wise **checklist**, registry-check links (council portals), duplicate-match panel, risk score, notes, decision (approve / needs-info / reject / escalate) |
| **Duplicate / fraud signals** | Same phone/email/PAN/GST/registration no./bank/IP/device, fuzzy name+address, doc hash reuse |
| **SLA timers** | Per type; breach alerts; auto-assign; workload balancing |
| **Needs-info loop** | Per-document comments, one-click resubmission request |
| **Probation controls** | Visibility cap, booking cap, payout holds for new providers |
| **Re-verification** | Expiry scheduler (60/30/7 days), annual review, complaint-triggered review |
| **Bulk tools** | Bulk approve low-risk (rule-based), bulk reminders |
| **Decision log** | Who/when/why — immutable, exportable |

## 3. Provider management
Search (name, phone, reg no.), status (pending, live, suspended, expired, banned), **suspend/unsuspend** (reason, duration, notify), **listing visibility controls** (hide, delist, geo-restrict), **claim-listing** requests (govt/imported), merge duplicates, branch/ownership transfer, performance panel (rating, cancel rate, complaints, response time), strike system (warnings → suspension → ban), payout hold toggle, impersonation **view-as** (read-only, audited, time-boxed, PHI masked).

## 4. Catalog & taxonomy management (extends ✅ Category admin)
- **Category tree editor** (drag/drop, parents, aliases, i18n names, icons, regulatory tags, `tier`, `adClaimsRestricted`) — see Subcategories doc Part B.
- **Specialty canonicalisation tool:** map free-text values → canonical codes; merge duplicates; review "Other (specify)" queue.
- **Test/medicine/product catalogue:** master catalogue, approvals for provider-added items, regulatory fields (Rx schedule, FSSAI/CDSCO), banned-ingredient/claim lists, batch/recall management.
- **Provider type configs** (join wizard JSON): enable/disable types per city, required docs, fees, agreements — versioned.
- **Service cities:** launch/pause categories per city ✅ (`ServiceCity`), pincode serviceability, delivery/home-visit radius defaults.

## 5. Content & UGC moderation

### 5.1 Queues
| Queue | Items | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| **Reviews & ratings** | Text, photos | Verified-booking only; abuse/PII/medical-claim/defamation filters; provider-reported; shadow-hide pending review |
| **Provider profiles & listings** | Bio, descriptions, photos, videos, before/after | Claim policy, copyright, consent for images, no contact details in text |
| **Q&A / community / support groups** | Posts, comments | Crisis keyword detection → escalate; no medical advice by non-clinicians; spam |
| **Events/camps** | Descriptions, banners | Organiser verification; partner facility proof; misleading "free test" claims |
| **Product listings** | Titles, images, claims | FSSAI/CDSCO checks, banned claims ("cures diabetes"), counterfeit signals |
| **Articles/videos** | Health content | Medical-review workflow (draft → medical reviewer → publish), citations, update cycle |
| **Chat reports** | Abuse/harassment flags | Limited context view, preserve evidence |
| **Images/uploads** | Profile/gallery | Auto-moderation (NSFW, face/ID leakage), manual review |

### 5.2 Tools
Auto-filters (keyword/regex/ML), severity scoring, **canned actions** (approve, edit-request, remove, warn, suspend), appeal flow, moderator notes, SLA, quality audits (second-reviewer sampling), reviewer wellbeing (blur sensitive images by default).

### 5.3 Policies to publish (Trust & Safety)
Community guidelines · Review policy · Advertising/claims policy (Drugs & Magic Remedies, Consumer Protection, ASCI) · Sensitive-content policy · Child-safety policy · Provider code of conduct · Anti-discrimination (incl. LGBTQ+, caste, gender) · Takedown/DMCA/IT Rules grievance officer.

## 6. Trust & Safety
| Area | Controls |
|---|---|
| **Fake provider detection** | Document/ID reuse, photo reuse, registration mismatch, address anomalies, device clustering |
| **Fake reviews** | Burst detection, same-device/IP rings, incentivised-review flags, verified-visit gate |
| **Home-visit / transport safety** | SOS ✅ handling, incident reports, panic alerts, trip anomalies, blacklist/denylist, mandatory police verification ✅ (attendants/nurses/drivers) |
| **Patient abuse of providers** | Harassment reports, ban/limit |
| **Fraud** | Coupon abuse, refund abuse, payment fraud (velocity, device), fake bookings, slot hoarding |
| **Crisis escalation** | Self-harm/violence keywords → human review queue (24x7 contact), helpline guidance, no auto-ban |
| **Child safety** | Detection, reporting, law-enforcement process |
| **Counterfeit/unsafe products** | Reports, recall, takedown, regulator liaison |
| **Data-breach / privacy incidents** | Incident workflow ✅ (docs), notifications timelines (DPDP/CERT-In) |

## 9. Marketing & growth (guardrails)

## 11. Compliance & Legal

## 12. Platform settings & feature flags
City/category launch toggles (Unleash ✅), fee/commission/cancellation defaults, rate limits, maintenance mode, emergency banner, notification templates (multilingual, sensitive-neutral variants), SLA configs, integrations (payments, SMS, maps, LiveKit), secrets rotation reminders (not values).

## 13. Audit & security of the console itself
- Mandatory 2FA/passkeys, short sessions, IP/device allowlist (optional), step-up on sensitive actions, **dual approval** (role→admin, bulk export, large payouts, legal takedown), **break-glass** access (reason, time-boxed, alert)
- **Immutable audit log** (who/what/when/why/before-after), alerts on anomalies (mass export, many PHI views, new admin)
- Reviewers see **minimum PHI** (masked, watermarked); no bulk download; session recording for high-risk actions (optional)
- Quarterly access reviews; offboarding revocation checklist.

## 14. Notifications & alerts for ops
Doc expiry spikes · SLA breaches · fraud spikes · safety incidents (SOS) · payout failures · moderation backlog · provider rating drop · category stockouts · system health ✅.
