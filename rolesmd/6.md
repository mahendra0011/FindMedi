# 06 — User (Patient) Dashboard: Changes & Additions

> **Existing (✅):** `PatientDashboard` — stat tiles (Upcoming Appointments, Active Prescriptions, Active Orders, Reports Ready, Notifications, Test Bookings, My Reviews), quick actions (Find Doctors, Home Visits, Video Consult, Voice Calls, Doctor Chat, Book Lab Test, Buy Medicine, Upload Report, Saved Doctors), sections (Health & Family Management, My Appointments, Payment History).
> **Pages (✅):** Addresses, Appointments, Assistants, Booking history, Care plan, Family, Favorites, Health ID (ABHA), Lawyers, Medicine orders/reminders, Payment, Prescriptions, Profile, Records, Referral, Refunds, Reports, Reviews, Rewards, Rides, Services, Settings, Support, Vitals.
>
> **Goal:** dashboard ko "doctor-booking app" se "**poore health ecosystem ka home**" banana, bina clutter ke.

---

## 1. New dashboard structure (top → bottom)

```
1. Greeting + profile switcher (Self / Family members ✅) + location/city chip + search bar
2. Alerts strip (urgent: upcoming in <2h, refill due, report ready, payment pending, document expiry)
3. "Today" card (next appointment, meds due, vitals due, live order/ride tracking)
4. Quick actions (personalised grid, 8 + "More")
5. My Health snapshot (vitals, care plan progress, health score*)
6. Upcoming (appointments, tests, home visits, classes, events)
7. Orders & deliveries (medicine, equipment rental, meals)
8. Programs & memberships (gym/yoga/diabetes program)
9. Family & caregivers
10. Records & reports (recent, shared, consent)
11. Discover (recommended for you, nearby, camps/events, packages)
12. Wallet · Rewards · Referral · Offers
13. Support & safety (SOS, helplines, grievances)
```
*Health score optional/opt-in; avoid anxiety-inducing scoring; no scores for sensitive conditions.

## 2. Section-wise details

### 2.1 Header / Global
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Profile switcher** | Self + family members ✅; all dashboard data re-scopes to selected person (with role-based access: child/elder as dependents) |
| **City/location chip** | `ServiceCity` ✅; "Use my location" (consent) |
| **Universal search** | Doctors, hospitals, labs, tests, medicines, dentists, yoga, gyms, dietitians, equipment, events, symptoms ("tooth pain") → category/provider suggestions; voice search; Hinglish + regional language; recent searches (opt-out) |
| **Notification bell** ✅ | Grouped (appointments, orders, reports, offers); discreet mode toggle |
| **Language switch** | Hindi/English/regional |

### 2.2 Alerts strip 🆕
Appointment starting soon · Join video call · Medicine refill due · Report ready ✅ · Payment pending · Doc/ID expiry (insurance, ABHA link) · Provider rescheduled/cancelled · Vaccination due (child) · Low wallet.

### 2.3 "Today" card 🆕
Timeline of the day: appointments, meds (tick to log ✅ MedicineReminder), vitals to log ✅, classes (yoga/gym), live delivery/ambulance/ride tracker, water/walk goals (opt-in).

### 2.4 Quick actions (personalised + expandable)
**Keep ✅:** Find Doctors · Book Lab Test · Buy Medicine · Video Consult · Upload Report · Saved.
**Add 🆕:** Dentist · Eye care · Physio · Dietitian · Mental health (discreet) · Home nursing · Rent equipment · Yoga & fitness · Camps & events · Vaccination · Health packages · Insurance/claims · Ambulance/SOS · Blood.
Grouped as **category tiles** (Doctors · Tests · Medicines · Care at home · Mind & body · Wellness · Elder care · Insurance) with "More" → full **Services** page ✅ (`PatientServices`).
Personalisation: most-used first; pin/unpin; hidden categories (privacy) setting.

### 2.5 My Health snapshot
| Card | Content |
|---|---|
| **Vitals** ✅ | Latest BP/sugar/weight/SpO2/temp, trend sparkline, "log now", device sync (P3) |
| **Care plan** ✅ | Chronic plan progress (diabetes, BP), next goals, next checkup |
| **Medicines** ✅ | Today's doses, adherence %, refill alerts, interactions warning |
| **Vaccinations** 🆕 | Child schedule (UIP+optional), adult boosters, due/overdue, nearest centre, certificate download |
| **Women's health** 🆕 (opt-in) | Cycle tracker, pregnancy week/visits, postpartum — **private by default**, separate consent |
| **Fitness** 🆕 (opt-in) | Steps/workouts (wearable/manual), class attendance, streaks, challenges (rewards ✅) |
| **Nutrition** 🆕 | Diet plan today, meal log, water, dietitian notes, grocery list |
| **Mental wellbeing** 🆕 (opt-in, private) | Mood check-in, sleep, session reminders, resources, **crisis help button** |
| **Allergies/conditions/blood group** 🆕 | Emergency card (shared in SOS with consent) |

### 2.6 Upcoming
Unified timeline (appointments ✅, test bookings ✅, home visits, physio sessions, classes, events, dialysis series ✅, vaccinations). Filters: person, type. Actions: join/check-in/reschedule/cancel/directions/add to calendar/prep instructions/upload docs.

### 2.7 Orders & deliveries
Medicine orders ✅ (status, track, reorder, return), equipment rentals 🆕 (active/return due/extend), meal subscriptions 🆕 (pause/skip), supplement/health-food orders 🆕, lab sample status/report ETA, ride/ambulance ✅. Reorder 1-tap, **refill reminders**.

### 2.8 Programs & memberships 🆕
Gym/yoga memberships (days left, freeze, renew, QR check-in), class packs (credits), chronic programs (milestones), physio packages (sessions left), meal plans. Auto-renew controls, invoices.

### 2.9 Family & caregivers ✅ (extend)
Family members ✅ with relation, age, consent, **dependent management**, caregiver (attendant ✅) booking, elder monitoring dashboard (meds, vitals, alerts to family), share-access with expiry (view-only), guardian consent for minors, emergency contacts.

### 2.10 Records & reports ✅ (extend)
Timeline (diagnoses, prescriptions, labs, imaging, discharge, vaccination, certificates 🆕), search, filters by person/type/date, **ABHA link** ✅, **consent manager** (who can see what, expiry, revoke, access log "who viewed my record"), upload external records, download/share securely (expiring link), trend charts for lab values 🆕, AI summary (opt-in, with disclaimer).

### 2.11 Discover (recommendations)
Near you: camps/events 🆕, vaccination drives, free screening; **health packages** ✅ by age/gender; trending doctors; saved/favorites ✅; "Based on your care plan" (diabetes → eye check, foot care, dietitian); seasonal alerts (dengue, AQI, heatwave); Jan Aushadhi/govt facilities 🆕; content (doctor-reviewed articles) ✅.
Rules: **no health-data-based ads**; personalisation on-device/consented; opt-out.

### 2.12 Wallet · Rewards · Referral ✅
Wallet balance, add money, refunds credited, **loyalty points** ✅, rewards ✅, referral ✅, coupons ✅, membership perks, expiry reminders.

### 2.13 Insurance & claims 🆕/🟡
Policies (add insurer/TPA, member IDs, validity), PM-JAY/CGHS/ESI cards, **claim tracker** (pre-auth, cashless, reimbursement) ✅ Insurance, documents vault, network hospital finder, bill-to-claim flow, claim status notifications.

### 2.14 Support & safety ✅
SOS ✅ (always visible), helpline directory (112, 108, 14416…), support tickets ✅, grievance ✅, patient-rights ✅, report a provider, chat with support, FAQs, feedback.

### 2.15 Settings & privacy ✅ (extend)
Profile, addresses ✅, languages, notifications (**discreet mode**), **privacy centre** (hide categories from recents/suggestions, who can see my records, data export/delete — DPDP rights), consents ledger, linked devices/sessions ✅, 2FA/passkeys, payment methods, accessibility (font size, high contrast, voice), data-saver mode.

## 4. Role-based variants of the same user app

| Persona | Emphasis |
|---|---|
| **Adult/Patient** | Appointments, meds, reports, insurance |
| **Parent** | Child profiles, vaccination, growth chart, paediatrician, school health |
| **Elderly user** | Large UI, voice, simple "Today", caregiver link, fall/SOS, med reminders; family co-manage |
| **Caregiver/family manager** | Multi-profile switcher, alerts for dependants, approvals |
| **Fitness/wellness user** | Classes, memberships, challenges, nutrition — minimal medical UI |
| **Chronic patient** | Care plan, vitals trends, refills, program coach |
| **Pregnant/new mother** | Pregnancy tracker, ANC visits, postpartum, baby vaccines |
Persona chosen at onboarding ("What brings you here?") — changes defaults only, not permissions.

## 5. First-time user onboarding (post-signup)
1. Language + city · 2. Who is this for (self/family) · 3. Interests (consult, tests, medicines, fitness, elder care…) · 4. Optional health profile (age, gender, blood group, conditions, allergies — skippable) · 5. Link ABHA (optional) · 6. Consents (explicit, granular) · 7. Notification prefs (incl. discreet) · 8. Tour + first recommended action.
Progress meter "Profile 60% complete" (not blocking).

## 6. Empty states & guidance
No appointments → "Find a doctor" + popular specialties · no records → "Upload or link ABHA" · no family → "Add family member" · no programs → recommended programs · offline → cached timeline + SOS numbers.

## 7. Performance & UX
Lazy-load sections, skeletons, cached last-known data, pull-to-refresh, bottom nav on mobile (Home · Search · Bookings · Records · Profile), SOS floating button, dark mode, low-bandwidth mode, accessibility AA.

## 8. API additions (summary)
`GET /patient/dashboard/summary?profileId=` (aggregated, cached short) · `GET /patient/timeline` · `GET/POST /patient/memberships` · `GET /patient/rentals` · `GET /patient/events` · `GET/PUT /patient/privacy` · `GET /patient/consents` + access-log · `GET /patient/recommendations` · `GET/POST /patient/insurance/policies`.
All endpoints: authn + object-level authz (self or authorised family), `no-store`, DTO allowlist, audit on record access.

## 9. Security & privacy
- [ ] Profile switching is **server-validated** (user ↔ dependant relationship), not client-sent ids
- [ ] Sensitive modules (mental, women's health, sexual health) hidden from notifications/recents by default; separate consent
- [ ] Access log visible to patient ("who viewed my records")
- [ ] Share links expiring + single-purpose; revoke
- [ ] Recommendation engine: no raw PHI to third parties/analytics; pseudonymised IDs
- [ ] Shared devices: auto-logout, clear query cache on logout ✅/to-do
- [ ] Data export/delete (DPDP) self-serve
