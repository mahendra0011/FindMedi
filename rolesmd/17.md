## 3. Pricing & fee design (principles)

1. **Transparent:** price breakup shown before pay (fee + platform fee + GST).
2. **Fee on provider side where possible** (commission), small/no patient platform fee early to drive adoption.
3. **Category-wise take-rates** (different effort/risk): clinical consults (lower), lab/diagnostics (low, price-sensitive), pharmacy (very thin margins), wellness/gym (higher), events (ticket fee), rental (medium).
4. **Plans:** Free (listing + limited bookings) → Basic (bookings, calendar, reminders) → Premium (analytics, featured credits, multi-staff, priority support).
5. **No pay-for-ranking in clinical trust signals** (verification/ratings never purchasable).
6. **Promotions:** funded jointly (platform/provider), caps to avoid burn; new-city launch offers time-boxed.
7. **Experiment** with flags ✅ (A/B take-rate by cohort, within fairness rules).
8. **Taxes:** GST on platform fee/commission; TDS on payouts (e.g., Section 194-O for e-commerce operators — **CA to confirm applicability**); TCS rules if marketplace collects on behalf of sellers.

## 5. Unit economics framework

### 5.1 Core formulas
```
GMV                = Σ transaction value (bookings + orders)
Take rate          = Net revenue from commissions / GMV
Revenue/booking    = AOV × take rate  (+ platform fee + ads share)
Variable cost/bkg  = gateway + notif + support + refund-leak + incentives + video/maps
Contribution/bkg   = Revenue/bkg − Variable cost/bkg
Patient LTV        = Contribution/bkg × bookings/yr × retention-years   (− reactivation cost)
Provider LTV       = Subscription + commission contribution over active life − success costs
CAC (patient)      = Patient acquisition spend / new paying patients
CAC (provider)     = Field+marketing+onboarding cost / activated providers
Payback (months)   = CAC / monthly contribution per customer
Break-even bkgs    = Fixed monthly costs / Contribution per booking (blended)
```

## 6. Go-to-market economics

### 6.1 Supply-side (providers)
- **Chicken-and-egg:** supply first (hand-onboard anchors), demand second.
- **Provider CAC levers:** field ops (salaried vs commission), referrals, associations (IMA/IDA chapters), pharmacist/lab networks, free tier + onboarding help.
- **Activation metric:** provider gets first booking within 14 days; **active provider** = ≥ N bookings/month.
- **Retention drivers:** bookings volume, payout speed, reminder/CRM value, support, fair commission.

### 6.2 Demand-side (patients)
- **Low-CAC channels:** SEO city/category pages, camps/events, WhatsApp referrals, RWAs/corporates, community pharmacies/labs QR, ASHA/Anganwadi/NGO tie-ups, repeat booking reminders.
- **Paid channels** (use sparingly): search ads for high-intent (dentist near me), social for wellness; no health-data-based targeting.
- **Retention:** records + reminders + family profiles (switching cost), memberships/programs, loyalty ✅, chronic plans.

### 6.3 City launch playbook economics
Per new city budget: field team (2–3) + local support + launch marketing + provider incentives + legal/regulatory local check. **Gate:** supply density (≥ X providers per category per Y km²), booking fill rate, repeat %, support load, payout reliability. Expand only when city #1 crosses thresholds.

## 8. Legal/commercial guardrails affecting the model

- **Doctor referral fees / fee-splitting:** Indian medical ethics rules restrict commissions for referrals. Structure platform charges as **technology/listing/convenience fees** with legal opinion; avoid "per-referral" payments to doctors.
- **Advertising by doctors:** NMC/state council advertising restrictions — your sponsored placements for doctors need legal review.
- **E-pharmacy rules:** evolving; ensure licensed pharmacy fulfilment and Rx compliance; don't hold Rx drug inventory without licences.
- **IRDAI:** membership plans that look like insurance can trigger regulation.
- **Payments:** if you hold funds/escrow (quotes, deposits), check RBI payment aggregator rules — typically use a licensed PA/escrow partner rather than holding funds.
- **GST/TDS/TCS:** correct treatment for healthcare vs wellness vs goods; invoice formats; CA.
- **Gig/labour:** riders, nurses, attendants — classification and social-security code implications.
- **Consumer law:** transparent pricing, refunds, no dark patterns.

## 12. Risks to the model & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Disintermediation (patient/provider go off-platform after 1st booking) | Value beyond booking (records, reminders, payments, reviews, CRM), follow-up bookings via platform, loyalty |
| Low take-rate categories (pharmacy/labs) | Use as traffic/retention; monetise via subscription/ads/B2B; avoid subsidising delivery |
| High CAC for patients | SEO, partnerships, camps, referrals; focus repeat |
| Supply quality | KYC, probation, reviews integrity, strikes |
| Regulatory shifts | Compliance register, advisors, modular categories |
| Payment/fraud losses | Risk engine, holds, reconciliation |
| Support cost explosion | Self-serve, templates, chatbot (safe), tiered support |
| Cash burn | Phase gates, 1-city focus, budgets/alerts |
| Dependence on a few big providers | Diversify supply, contracts, multi-branch tools |
