# NMC/state council, Bar Council, IRDAI advertising rules (draft)

## NMC (doctors)
- No solicitation of patients; no guarantee of cure.
- Telemedicine per NMC Telemedicine Guidelines 2020 (consent, ID, prescribing limits).
- Professional qualifications must be accurate.

## Bar Council (lawyers)
- No advertising of legal services beyond permitted informational content.
- No solicitation or touting.

## IRDAI (insurance)
- Insurance messaging must be factual; no misleading benefit claims.
- Disclosure of terms, exclusions, and grievance channel.

## FindMedi controls
- Provider listings are informational, not promotional.
- `adClaimsRestricted` flags + claims moderation.
- Lawyer listings use discreet, non-solicitation copy.
