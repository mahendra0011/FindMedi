# Reputation/crisis comms playbook (draft)

## Severity levels
- P1 (critical): data breach, patient harm, legal/regulatory. Response: <1h.
- P2 (high): service outage, viral negative. Response: <4h.
- P3 (medium): isolated complaint, minor bug. Response: <24h.

## Steps
1. Detect (monitoring/alerts/social listening).
2. Assess (severity, scope, stakeholders).
3. Respond (holding statement, internal comms, customer comms).
4. Resolve (fix, compensate, follow-up).
5. Review (post-mortem, policy update).

## Templates
- Holding statement, apology, resolution, update.
- Channel: email, SMS, in-app, social, press.

## Contacts
- Spokesperson, legal, clinical advisor, DPO, ops lead.
