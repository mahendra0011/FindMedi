# Partnership kits + camp playbook (draft)

## Partnership kit contents
- One-pager (value prop, audience, metrics).
- Co-branded landing page template.
- Onboarding checklist for partner providers.
- Revenue-share terms template.
- Tracking: UTM links, referral codes.

## Camp playbook
1. Identify venue + partner (NGO/RWA/corporate).
2. Define services (screening, vaccination, awareness).
3. Register event (Event model, capacity, fee).
4. Promote (WhatsApp, SMS, local).
5. Execute (check-in, ticket QR, consent).
6. Follow-up (results delivery, feedback, report).

## First 10 partners
- Target: 2 hospitals, 2 labs, 2 pharmacies, 2 NGOs, 2 corporate wellness.
