# ASCI & Consumer Protection guidelines for claims/endorsements (draft)

## Key rules (ASCI Code + Consumer Protection Act 2019)
- No misleading or unsubstantiated claims.
- Health claims need scientific backing; "cure" claims prohibited (Drugs & Magic Remedies Act).
- Influencer endorsements must disclose paid partnership (#ad, #sponsored).
- Testimonials require consent; no patient data in creatives.
- Comparisons must be factual and verifiable.

## FindMedi controls
- `adClaimsRestricted` taxonomy flags on cosmetic/weight-loss/sexual-health/IVF categories.
- Claims moderation severity raised for restricted categories.
- Dual approval for any success-rate/cure claim (adminApplications two-person lane).
- Sponsored cards carry explicit sponsored label (`isSponsored`).

## Review workflow
- Marketing draft -> clinical advisor review -> legal review (for sensitive) -> publish.
- All claims logged in compliance register.
