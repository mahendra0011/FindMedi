# Review & claims approval workflow (draft)

## Workflow
1. Marketing drafts copy/claim.
2. Clinical advisor reviews medical claims (especially sensitive categories).
3. Legal reviews (ASCI, Consumer Protection, NMC/Bar Council/IRDAI).
4. Approve/reject with comments.
5. Publish only after all approvals.

## Controls
- `adClaimsRestricted` categories require dual approval.
- Claims moderation severity raised for restricted categories.
- All claims logged in compliance register.
- Sponsored content labelled.
