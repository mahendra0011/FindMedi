# Positioning & messaging (draft)

## Positioning
FindMedi: India's trusted healthcare discovery and booking platform — from specialists to home care, with privacy-first design.

## Key messages
- Find the right specialist, fast.
- Book in-person, video, or home visit.
- Your health data stays private.
- Verified providers, transparent pricing.

## Brand guidelines (draft)
- Primary: teal (brand). Accent: amber (urgent). Neutral grays.
- Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans (headings), Inter (body), Noto Sans (Indic).
- Tone: warm, clear, trustworthy, non-alarming.
- Asset kit: logo variants, color tokens, icon set (lucide).
