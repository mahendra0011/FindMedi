# Consent for testimonials/photos (draft)

## Consent form (template)
- I, [Name], consent to FindMedi using my testimonial/photo in marketing materials.
- I understand: (1) my name/photo may be published, (2) I can revoke consent anytime, (3) no patient data/medical details will be shared, (4) no compensation unless stated.

## Rules
- Written consent required before publishing any testimonial or photo.
- No patient data (diagnosis, treatment details) in creatives.
- Sponsored content must be labelled (#ad, #sponsored).
- Sensitive category content (mental health, sexual health, IVF) requires extra review.
