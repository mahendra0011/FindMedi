# Take-rate/plan pricing hypotheses (draft)

## Hypotheses
- H1: 10-15% commission on appointments is sustainable for providers.
- H2: Subscription plans (basic/premium) reduce churn and increase LTV.
- H3: Free tier for providers drives supply; conversion to paid at 100+ bookings/mo.
- H4: Patient-side convenience fee (Rs 20-50) acceptable for home visits.

## Experiment plan
- A/B test commission rates by city (10% vs 15%).
- Offer 3-month free premium to first 50 providers per city.
- Track activation, retention, revenue per provider, patient repeat rate.
- Quarterly pricing review with finance.
