# Patient FAQ (draft)

## Finding care
- Browse by specialty at /browse-specialties (43 specialties, 300 sub-specialties).
- Filter by city, mode (in-person/video/home), fee, language, gender, open now, schemes.

## Booking
- Book appointments, home visits, lab tests, pharmacy orders, rentals, memberships, events.
- Payments via card/UPI/netbanking/wallet; refunds per cancellation policy snapshot at booking.

## Privacy
- Sensitive modules (mental, women's, sexual health) hidden from notifications/recents by default.
- Access log shows who viewed your records: /api/access-log.
- Data export/delete (DPDP) self-serve via /api/dsr.

## Family
- Add family members; switch profiles (server-validated).
- Emergency card: allergies, conditions, blood group, contacts.

## Reviews
- Reviews require a verified visit (bookingRef). Rate-limited; abuse/PII/claim/crisis detection.

## Help
- Helplines: /helplines. Support tickets: /support. Grievance officer contact in footer.
