# Short-form (plain-language) policy summaries (draft)

## Terms of Service — plain summary
FindMedi connects patients with healthcare providers. By using the app you agree to: (1) provide accurate info, (2) use services responsibly, (3) not misuse reviews or data. Providers agree to verified listings and fair pricing. Full ToS applies.

## Privacy Policy — plain summary
We collect only what's needed to deliver care. Your health data is encrypted and never sold. You can view, export, or delete your data anytime. Sensitive health info is hidden from notifications by default.

## Refund Policy — plain summary
Refunds follow the cancellation policy shown at booking. Provider-side cancellations are fully refunded. Patient cancellations depend on timing. Disputes are reviewed within 48h.

## Provider Agreement — plain summary
Providers agree to: verified credentials, fair pricing, timely service, no false claims. FindMedi takes a commission per booking. Violations can lead to suspension.
