# Bottom-up market sizing (sourced, 2026)

## Total addressable market (India healthcare)
- India healthcare market: ~USD 400B in FY26 (InsightRx, 2026), growing 8-12% annually; organised/investable segment ~USD 220-250B.
- India hospital market: ~USD 193B in 2025, projected ~USD 365B by 2034 at ~8% CAGR (InsightRx; Sharekhan projects USD 364.6B by 2034 at 7.2% CAGR).
- India diagnostics: ~USD 40B in 2025, ~12.7% CAGR to ~USD 124B by 2034 (InsightRx).
- India e-pharmacy: USD 3.5B in 2025, 16.18% CAGR to USD 8.6B by 2031 (Ken Research).
- Digital health: ~USD 17.8B in 2025, ~25% CAGR to ~USD 107B by 2033; telehealth ~45% of revenue (InsightRx).
- Medical tourism: ~USD 8.7-9B annually (IBEF 2025; InsightRx).

## Serviceable addressable market (FindMedi beachhead)
- Urban Tier-1/Tier-2 cities, out-of-pocket + insured patients seeking discovery/booking.
- Beachhead: Jabalpur/Lucknow-class cities → ~50M population in top 20 cities.
- Assumption: 5% of urban healthcare spend addressable via discovery platform = ~USD 2-3B.

## Serviceable obtainable market (5-year)
- Target: 1% of SAM by Year 5 = ~USD 20-30M GMV, at 10-15% take rate = ~USD 2-4M revenue.
