## Serviceable addressable market (FindMedi beachhead)

- Urban Tier-1/Tier-2 cities, out-of-pocket + insured demand for discovery/booking.
- Initial beachhead: Jabalpur/Lucknow-class cities — top-20 tier-1/2 cities reach ~50M+ population.
- Working assumption: **5% of urban healthcare spend is addressable through a verified discovery + booking platform = ~US$ 2–3B SAM.**

## Serviceable obtainable market (5-year)

- 1% of SAM by Year 5 ≈ **US$ 20–30M GMV**; at 10–15% take rate ≈ **US$ 2–4M platform revenue**.
