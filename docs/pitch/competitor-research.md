# Competitor research — Indian healthcare discovery platforms (2026)

## Key competitors
| Platform | Strengths | Weaknesses | FindMedi differentiation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tata 1mg | E-pharmacy + labs + teleconsult; 20,000+ pincodes; Tata trust | Pharmacy-led; limited offline clinical integration | Full provider-type coverage (20+ types), facility detail pages, discreet mode for sensitive |
| Apollo 24\|7 | Fastest delivery (19 min); hospital tie-in; pharmacy+diagnostics+consult | Metro-focused; corporate hospital bias | Tier-2/3 focus, claim-listing for govt facilities, field ops |
| PharmEasy | Largest pharmacy catalogue; home healthcare | Pharmacy-first; weaker discovery | Unified discovery across 43 specialties + facility types |
| Practo | Doctor consult + appointments + pharmacy | Weaker pharmacy; no home care | Home care, rentals, memberships, events, community |
| MediBuddy | Enterprise/corporate wellness; tier-2 | B2B focus | B2C discovery + B2B corporate wellness |
| Netmeds | Reliance ecosystem; Jio integration | Pharmacy-only | Full-stack discovery |
| Lybrate/Portea | Home care (Portea) | Narrow | Integrated home care + facility discovery |

## Gaps FindMedi targets
- Tier-2/3 city coverage and govt facility claim-listings.
- Discreet mode for sensitive categories (mental/sexual health, IVF, de-addiction).
- Config-driven onboarding for 20+ provider types (not just pharmacy).
- Facility-type detail pages (26 types) with SEO.
- Community, courses, seasonal alerts, govt schemes, myth-busting (content moat).
