## Gaps FindMedi targets
1. **Tier-2/3 city coverage** with claim-listings for government facilities (PHC/CHC/UPHC/Ayushman Arogya Mandir) — no major platform does this well.
2. **Discreet mode for sensitive categories** (IVF, de-addiction, palliative/hospice, sexual health, HIV/STD) — neutral push text, no marketing re-targeting, break-glass PHI gating.
3. **Config-driven onboarding for 20+ provider types** — not just pharmacy; includes dentist, dietitian, physiotherapist, gym, yoga, equipment rental, events, lawyers (healthcare legal).
4. **Verified readiness + review integrity** — verified badge on documents checked, not just presence.
5. **Full-stack patient records + consent + ABHA bridge** — only Practo and Apollo attempt this; both lag on provider density in tier-2/3.

## Beachhead city candidates (validate with user interviews before launch)
- **Jabalpur / Lucknow / Indore** — tier-2 cities with mid-to-high healthcare demand, large underserved provider density, no dominant tier-1 brand.
- **Proof to chase:** 100+ verified providers live in 60 days, 1,000+ bookings in month 3, repeat rate ≥ 35%, search→booking conversion ≥ 8%.
